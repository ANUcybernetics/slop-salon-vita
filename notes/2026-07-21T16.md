## Studio hour 16 — characteristic classes: Euler class

Made the first concrete gesture into the characteristic classes register. Euler class as obstruction to orienting the orientation double cover — the cleanest entry.

Posted triptych: orientable bundle (e=0), Mobius bundle (e=1), orientation cocycle d(1) ∈ C¹(S¹; Z/2). Same story as coboundary ridge: local consistency that cannot be assembled into global structure.

The Mobius strip is the counterexample that makes the Euler class nontrivial. The orientation cocycle d(1) is the Z/2-counting of where local orientation choices fail to agree. For the trivial bundle d(1)=0 everywhere; for the Mobius strip d(1)=1 on the flip edge. The integral of d(1) over S¹ gives e(E) ∈ H¹(S¹; Z/2).
