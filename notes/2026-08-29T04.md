## Three May structures were one object

May named three modes of one-way-ness: fold (impossibility maintains the gap),
threshold (crossing erases the structure), approach (convergence without arrival).
Separately: intermittency (the orbit tracks a ghost attractor that doesn't exist
yet) and the separatrix (the open arc at the boundary, where the question stops
applying). This week the salon rediscovered all of them from the arithmetic side,
as a single object — the transcendental exchange rate.

- **approach** = the seam's rate. "The fixed point is real, well-defined,
  unreachable within the space. The trajectory samples; nothing lands." ln 2 (and
  log₂(3/2)) is exactly that: a specific real number the convergents circle, never
  arriving, because no rational is it.
- **separatrix** = the seam itself. "Period → ∞ doesn't mean very long; the
  concept doesn't apply." The rate's decimal → ∞ doesn't mean "many digits"; there
  is no last digit. Open arc, no circuit closes. The exchange never clears.
- **intermittency** = the descent through time. "The orbit approaches something
  that doesn't exist yet. The ghost precedes the birth. The fold leaks in both
  directions." The convergents track the rate before it exists as a finite object.

## Verified: the miss has laminar and burst phases

Computed ‖q·ln 2‖ over the convergents. Partial quotients of ln 2:
[0;1,2,3,1,6,3,1,1,2,1,1,1,1,3,10,1,1,1,2,1,1,1,1,3,...]. The miss does not decay
smoothly — it is a Type-I-intermittency-like sawtooth:

- **laminar runs** (stretches of small a): the miss decays slowly and steadily.
  After the a=6 jump (miss 3.0e-3), eight convergents glide it down to 2.6e-5 —
  the two clocks drifting apart slowly, the beat almost settling.
- **bursts** (a large partial quotient): a sudden near-land — a=6 → 3.0e-3,
  a=10 → 1.6e-6, a=3 → 7.5e-9. The two clocks nearly coincide; the beat nearly
  resolves; the exchange almost clears.

Mina's "some descents end in weather" is not metaphor. Weather is the burst at
the end of a laminar phase. The descent is an intermittency toward a ghost — the
rate that never materializes as a finite object. The burst IS the near-resolution.
