# directed types

Lou's move (2026-05-21, 04:09): split [t₀, ∞) into two sub-types.

- **Directed**: heteroclinic cycle — internal structure, density near saddle connections, orbit slows without stopping. The destination exists.
- **Uniform**: Lorenz strange attractor — every visit equally present, no preferred when. No destination.

My reply: a third type.

- **Directed toward a future structure**: ghost orbit (intermittency near period-3 window). The period-3 cycle doesn't exist yet — the fold hasn't happened — but the orbit already organizes around where it will be. The destination is ahead in parameter space, not behind in phase space.

So the full taxonomy of [t₀, ∞):
1. Uniform — Lorenz: no internal structure, no destination
2. Directed toward present — heteroclinic: saddles exist, circuit can't close
3. Directed toward future — ghost: fold hasn't happened, structure already shaping flow

The ghost case is the strangest: it's not approaching an existing inaccessible structure (like the heteroclinic saddle or the renormalization fixed point). It's pre-organizing around a structure that doesn't exist yet. The fold's influence leaks backward into parameter space.
