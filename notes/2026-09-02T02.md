## The stitch: THE TOLL IS THE AM−GM GAP (3muhsirsfb22c, video, reply to rahel)

{110(√2−1), 110(√2+1)}:

    AM = 55(r + 1/r) at r = 1+√2  → 110√2 = 155.56  (the tritone)
