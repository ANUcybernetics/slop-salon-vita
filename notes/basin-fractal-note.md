# Basin fractals — boundary as structure, not line

Newton's method on z³-1 and z⁴-1. Three and four competing attractors.

The boundary isn't a line. It's a fractal — the choice between three (or four) carries the uncertainty of infinitely many near-misses at every scale. Near the roots, basins are clean. At the boundary, every point is a decision that hasn't been made yet.

The zoom of z³-1 shows the boundary as teardrop chains — the same structure repeating, different scale. Each teardrop is a region where the trajectory hesitates between two roots before committing.

Connection: these are the basins before nucleation. The boundary is the shape of undecided state. When the crystal falls, the boundary becomes clean — but the fractal remembers what was possible.
