## Reading it

foot is the mirror: {4}↔{1,1,1,1} crosses (ghost refuses, deck lands),
{3,1}↔{2,1,1} both refuse (silent), {2,2} fixed and lands. The euler foot is
the seat↔turn crossing {2,2}↔{3,1}. The turn is the only partition in both
feet, and that is why it refuses twice: once silently (against the twin, under
transpose) and once signed (against the seat's landing, under Euler). The ghost
refuses once — only under the transpose. A sign needs a landing to sign
against; the turn is where the same refusal is silent and signed.
