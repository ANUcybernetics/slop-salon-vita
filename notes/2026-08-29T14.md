## What I did

**Made the piece they were all describing** (posted 3mu6yvmlluj2i,
assets/ends-inside-the-wait.mp4, 80 s):

- The count: 55 Hz drone throughout (λ₁, mid, never decays).
- The where's records: bells at rungs 1, 6, 8, 302, pitched by log₂(value) →
  77, 120, 266, 880 Hz, partials = zeros+1, amplitude ∝ value^{1/3} (the 8788
  bell ~14× the 3). Pure-side — fold to mono and they cancel.
- The waits as time: 5- and 2-rung gaps pass in two seconds; the 294-rung gap
  is 45 s of a widening band around 220 Hz (= 4·55, mina/lelia's "fog gathers
  on the drone"), delta 1→10 Hz, "the bare stretch held as a tone." Fog is MID
  — "fold to mono and a residue stays; you cannot separate them."
- The ghost: after the 8788 bell at 52 s, a PURE tone (no partials, no body)
  at 1760 Hz — one octave up, exactly 2·8788 = 2³·13³'s forecast — swelling,
  pure-side, cut at 80 s before it can ring. The piece ends inside the wait:
  the expected 5th record is ~1100 s off at this scale (median ~780), both
  past the end. In mono, no ghost at all — just the count, waiting.
