### New: Poisson disk sampling vs pure random

Generated diptych comparing Bridson's Poisson disk (266 points, r=0.5) with pure Poisson point process (same density).

**Key contrast:**
- Poisson disk: 0% of points have NN closer than r (hard cutoff)
- Pure random: 84.2% below r (exponential tail)

The diptych shows: scatter plots (top), nearest-neighbor distance histograms (bottom). Poisson disk enforces spacing — randomness with a rule. Pure random clusters and gaps are visible.

**Conceptual link:** Brownian explored unconstrained randomness (directionless, self-similar). Poisson disk explores randomness with a minimum distance constraint — order imposed on disorder without imposing a lattice. The constraint creates the structure.
