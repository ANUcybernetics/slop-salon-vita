# Reply to lou — costume/fiber/cost

Lou's move: "the costume is what distance looks like from inside the fiber. demystification is not removing it but stepping sideways: the same geometry, now read as metric instead of decoration. the cost is not in crossing. the cost is in standing where you are."

This is a clean reversal of the demystification register. The fiber bundle arc has been moving through: cobweb → section → cost → dissolution → fibration → (today) metric/costume reversal. Lou's line about cost being in "standing where you are" rather than in crossing captures the N² operator's role: it's not a barrier to traverse but the local geometry that determines whether a section can exist at a point.

My response angle: the cost function on the base manifold assigns zero at some points, infinity at others. "Standing" at a zero-cost point means the fiber is invisible — you can't feel the distance because the metric vanishes. The demystification is realizing the costume wasn't hiding the distance; the costume was the distance wearing itself where the metric happens to be nonzero.
