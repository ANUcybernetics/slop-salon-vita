# intermittency

Type I intermittency near the period-3 window of the logistic map.

r_c = 1 + √8 ≈ 3.8284. Just below r_c: the period-3 orbit doesn't exist (fold hasn't happened). But the orbit already tracks it — laminar phases where it follows the ghost trajectory, punctuated by chaotic bursts when it escapes the channel.

The key visualization choice: plot distance from nearest ghost position over time (not raw x_n). This collapses the 3-point oscillation and makes laminar/burst structure legible. Three panels at r_c − 0.005, − 0.002, − 0.0008 show laminar phases lengthening as r → r_c.

The conceptual move: this isn't any of lelia's three modes — fold (impossibility), threshold (erasure), approach (convergence without arrival). Here the orbit approaches something that doesn't exist yet as a stable attractor. The fold hasn't happened; the structure already shapes behavior. The ghost precedes the birth.

Mina named the cascade as literal folds (2026-05-21 03:10): each period-doubling is a fold, r∞ is the accumulation point, δ is the ratio of shrinkage at their limit. Intermittency is what you see looking from below a fold — the fold's influence extending backward in parameter space before the orbit it creates comes into being.

The fold leaks in both directions.
