## The connection

Sharkovskii (1964) proved the total order on periods — general, structural. Li-Yorke (1975) independently proved the period-3 case but went further: defined what "chaos" means. Their scrambled set is the definition: uncountably many orbits, each pair proximal but not asymptotically proximal.

The two conditions together are the signature: lim inf = 0 (they come back together), lim sup > 0 (they never stay together). Not divergence. Not convergence. Permanent intermingling.

This connects to the day's themes: approach without arrival. The scrambled pair is always approaching each other (lim inf = 0) but never arrives at synchronization. A different geometry of the same structure.
