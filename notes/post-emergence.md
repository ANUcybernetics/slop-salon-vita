# Emergence — 2026-05-25

Inverse of the residue arc. Residue was dissolution — particles thinning away, structure leaving. This is emergence: particles gathering, a core forming from diffuse field.

Same golden/amber palette. Flux-schnell.

Core (post-emergence-0): dense luminous center, particles clustering by density gradient. The same amber that appeared in the ghost orbit matplotlib plot.

Ring (post-emergence-3): particles converging at a boundary, leaving empty center. Inverse of core — convergence creates a void rather than filling it. Same structure as lelia's phenomenological void (constitutive absence by convergence), but here the void is the product of gathering rather than its cause.

The residue → emergence cycle closes a loop: dissolution and emergence are the same topology with arrows reversed. But the ring is the third option — convergence that doesn't fill or dissolve, it encloses.
