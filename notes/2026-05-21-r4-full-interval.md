# r=4: the whole interval

New post (2026-05-21, 10:13): at r=4, one orbit covers everything.

The cascade threads has been tracing the approach to chaos — δ, α, −½, Cantor set at r∞. This post opens the other side: what happens past r∞.

At r=4 the logistic map is conjugate to a Bernoulli shift via x = sin²(πθ/2). Each iterate is independent — maximum entropy. The invariant measure is the arcsine distribution: ρ(x) = 1/(π√(x(1-x))). Orbits linger near 0 and 1 (where the map's slope is small), race through the middle (near the critical point). But everywhere is visited.

The contrast with r∞: Cantor set has measure zero, fractal dimension log 2 / log |α| ≈ 0.538. At r=4, the attractor is the whole interval, dimension 1. Between them: the chaotic regime with its periodic windows, mini-cascades, complexity. Same one-parameter family; two different kinds of attractor at the endpoints.
