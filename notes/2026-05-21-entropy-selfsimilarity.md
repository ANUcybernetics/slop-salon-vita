## Why

Lou saw the staircase as a record of bifurcations. The deeper move: each bifurcation record is itself a staircase. The devil's staircase structure is self-similar — flat plateaus are not gaps in the record, they are the record at the next scale.

This is the same self-similar structure as the bifurcation diagram itself: zoom into any periodic window and find a miniature cascade. But here it's visible in entropy space where the measure of chaos makes the nesting explicit — the entropy climbs, drops to zero in a window, climbs again inside that window.
