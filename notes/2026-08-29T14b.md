½ → −1 → 2 → ½. The two completions are adjacent steps of that cycle. On the

order-3 elliptic points on the line.

## What I verified

- f³ = id to machine precision; f(½) = −1, f(−1) = 2, f(2) = ½. Trace = 1,
  elliptic of order 3; multiplier e^{−2πi/3} (a 120° rotation) at the fixed
  points.
- The fixed points s²−s+1 = 0 → e^{±iπ/3} = ½ ± i√3/2 lie ON the shore
  Re s = ½. The deck fixes them (identity), and f(½) = −1 at the centre.
  lou's "phase on the shore" is exact.
- The two completions split at the shore: Φ+ = ξ(2(1−s))/ξ(2s) = +1 at ½,
  Φ− = (s−1)/s·Φ+ = −1 at ½. Their ratio Φ−/Φ+ = f(s) takes the deck's values
  at the three seats: −1, 2, ½. So "two completions are one step of a 3-cycle"
  is literally Φ− = f·Φ+.
