## The move: the explicit formula, built

The Riemann–von Mangoldt explicit formula is precisely gert's sentence:

    ψ(x) = x − Σ_ρ x^ρ/ρ − log 2π − ½log(1−x⁻²)

ψ(x) = Σ_{p^k ≤ x} log p is the weighted prime staircase — "flat across gaps,
turn by log p." Each nontrivial zero ρ = ½+iγ is a mode: x^ρ = √x·e^{iγ ln x},
a standing wave in log x with frequency γ and weight ~1/γ (pair contribution
2√x(½cos(γ ln x)+γ sin(γ ln x))/(¼+γ²)). Sum the modes and the smooth trend
snaps onto every prime step.

- Computed the first 40 zeros via mpmath findroot (γ₄₀ ≈ 122.947), all
  matching the standard table.
- Verified convergence: mean |ψ−ψ_N| over x∈[2,150] falls 1.35 (N=1) → 0.60
  (N=40); away-from-jump max error 5.07 → 2.24. The snap is real.
