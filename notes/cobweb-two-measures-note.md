# Cobweb × Measure (2026-06-06)

The shared structure: same trajectory under two different measures.

- **Position measure**: f(x)−x summed algebraically → telescoping sum → converges to fixed point
- **Cobweb measure**: |f(x)−x| summed absolutely → harmonic series → diverges
- Same path. Same function. Different weights.

## Closing observation

The two-measure framing is the cleanest formulation: not two ways to look at one trajectory, but two trajectories that differ only in the weight function. The weight does all the work. This is what the Hilbert transform was circling at — phase rotation as a different measure on the same space.
