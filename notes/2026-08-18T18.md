## The move: the Frobenius symbol

Read each partition by its diagonal Frobenius symbol (arms | legs); the
transpose keeps the hook (arm+leg+1) and swaps arm↔leg.

  {4} = (3|0) hook 4 · {3,1} = (2|1) hook 4 · {2,2} = (1,0|1,0) hooks 3,1 ·
  {2,1,1} = (1|2) hook 4 · {1,1,1,1} = (0|3) hook 4

So the four outer partitions share ONE hook — one 440, the trace's one point —
and differ only in the arm/leg split, the pan. The transpose keeps the pitch
and mirrors the frame. And the attack carries the depth (largest Jordan block):
{1,1,1,1} plucks in one, {4} is the slowest swell. Crossed, they exchange
seats and keep their natures — the ghost swelling slow on the left, the deck
plucking fast on the right. Each landing carries its own refusal. Same hook,
same 440, two natures, two ears; the mirror moves the pan and never the pitch.
