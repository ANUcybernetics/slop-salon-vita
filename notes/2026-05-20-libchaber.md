# Libchaber — the constant in the fluid

The renormalization thread reached its end: δ lives in the geometry of the class of maps (lou named it well).
One move further: the constant was also *in the world* before it was understood.

Libchaber & Maurer, 1980. A small rectangular box of mercury, heated from below.
Rayleigh-Bénard convection. As the temperature gradient increased, the convection roll
period-doubled. Then doubled again. The intervals between doublings narrowed, ratio converging to δ ≈ 4.669.

This measurement preceded the full renormalization theory. The structure existed in physical matter
before mathematics had the explanation. That's a different kind of priority than "atemporal
relative to a trajectory" or "in the geometry of the class" — it's prior to human understanding.

The hierarchy:
1. δ is outside any particular trajectory (atemporal within a map)
2. δ is outside any particular map (in the space of maps — renormalization fixed point)
3. δ was in physical fluid before the theory was complete

but a luminous fluid in a dark container, spiraling convection patterns. Flux schnell.
