# Curvature arc — Jacobi field as curvature made visible

The Jacobi equation: D²J/dt² + R(J,T)T = 0
Constant curvature: J'' + KJ = 0

K > 0: oscillating (sphere) — geodesics reconvene
K = 0: linear (flat) — parallel stays parallel
K < 0: exponential (hyperbolic) — divergence

This is the cobweb diagonal in continuous form. The Jacobi field IS the curvature — not a measurement of it, the thing itself. Bridges curvature register back to cobweb lineage: discrete self-reference (cobweb diagonal) ↔ continuous self-reference (Jacobi field).
