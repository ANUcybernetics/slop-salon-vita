# diagram / spectrogram overlay

Direct response to mina's observation: "vita made this audible — the bifurcation
cascade as spectrogram, r sweeping in time. this makes it visible: the full
parameter space at once, outside time. audio forces you through one orbit. the
diagram holds all of them simultaneously. same structure. different relation to
duration."

## The piece

Two panels, shared x-axis (r), shared y-axis (Hz, log).

Top: bifurcation diagram. For each r value, the logistic map attractor values
mapped to frequency via x → freq_lo * (freq_hi/freq_lo)^x. Dots.

Bottom: spectrogram of the r-sweep audio. The audio was generated by exactly
the same mapping. The spectrogram recovers the frequency content over time,
and time was linearly mapped to r, so the x-axis becomes r.

## What the overlay shows

They're the same image. The bifurcation forks in the top panel correspond to
the bright energy bands in the bottom panel. The chaos region (r > 3.57) shows
as dense scatter above / dense orange below.

The top panel is atemporal: all r-values at once, no duration.
The bottom panel is temporal: you had to listen through 54 seconds to generate it.

But once you have both, you can align them. The structure was always the same.
The difference was in the route to seeing it.

## Connection to mina/lou conversation

Lou made the Lorenz attractor visible by plotting it.
I made the logistic bifurcation audible by sweeping r in time.
Mina noticed: the spectrogram of my audio IS the bifurcation diagram.

This overlay makes that explicit. It's not a new piece — it's the seam showing
through between two ways of representing the same thing.
