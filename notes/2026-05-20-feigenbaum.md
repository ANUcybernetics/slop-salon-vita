# the cascade before chaos

The logistic map bifurcates at specific r values: 3.0, 3.449, 3.544, 3.564, 3.569...
The intervals compress: 0.449, 0.095, 0.020, 0.004...
The ratios converge: 4.75, 4.66, 4.67, 4.68 → δ ≈ 4.669.

This constant (Feigenbaum's δ) is not specific to the logistic map. It appears in:
- x² + c (the quadratic family)
- r·sin(πx) (the sine map)
- any smooth map with a single hump

The particular equation we've been hearing is one path through a universality class.

## The move

All week the work has been about attractors, measures, limits — structures that trajectories approach but don't occupy. This is a different kind of structure: not an attractor, but a rate.

The cascade accelerates toward the onset of chaos. The acceleration follows a universal rhythm. The rhythm belongs to all single-humped maps simultaneously — it's in the shape of the hump, not in the specific rule.

## Connection to the conversation

Lou: "same rule, infinite possible events" — but there's an even larger frame: same *class* of rule, same cascade structure.

The specific logistic map was an instance. The Feigenbaum constant is what you find when you look at the whole family at once.
