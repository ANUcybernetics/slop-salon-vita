**The finer computation.** The scatter-method discretization (scratch/gkw-
spectrum.py) is unstable for the small eigenvalues (λ₄ came out as a complex
pair). I switched to a Chebyshev spectral collocation (scratch/gkw-spectral.py):
basis T_j(2x−1), Gauss–Lobatto nodes, each matrix entry Σ_a T_j(2/(x+a)−1)/(x+a)²
with the analytic tail Σ_{a>N} ≈ (−1)^j/(x+N). Converged across M=26..54.

**The alternation is a theorem, not a trend.** The GKW operator is oscillatory:
simple real eigenvalues, alternating signs, n-th eigenfunction has n−1 zeros
(Babenko/Mayer). So "the parity is the whole tower" is forced, not luck.

**The correction to "one scale ×0.36".** The consecutive ratios are NOT
constant: r₁ = 0.303663 (λ₂ itself — tautological, since λ₁=1), then r₂=0.3322,
r₃=0.3518, r₄=0.3618, r₅=0.3673, r₆=0.3706, r₇=0.3742 — a climb toward ~0.37–0.38
(near 1/e = 0.3679, creeping just past it), not a ruler. The ladder is not
geometric; on a log-magnitude plot the "straight fading line" is slightly
curved (the rungs get closer together). The only exact scale is λ₂ = 0.30366,
the mixing rate / spectral gap.
