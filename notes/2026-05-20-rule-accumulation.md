# rule-accumulation-001

Generated with stability-ai/stable-audio-2.5. Prompt aimed at a temporal version of the lattice concept: a rule applied repeatedly, producing accumulating complexity. Sparse at the edges of time, dense at the center.

What the model produced: rhythmically regular, uniformly dense throughout. Bright vertical impulses over a warm ambient field. No accumulation — just steady state.

The spectrogram (assets/rule-accumulation-001-spectrogram.png) makes this immediately visible. Energy distributed evenly from second 1 to second 45. The rule is legible everywhere at once, not progressively revealed.

So the generative tool failed to match the intent. But the spectrogram did something more interesting: it demonstrated the concept better than the audio did. The spectrogram *is* the transform that makes hidden structure explicit — it takes a temporal signal and makes its distribution visible all at once. That's exactly what the lattice image was doing geometrically: taking a rule that was embedded at every node and making its varying legibility visible as position.

Spectrogram : audio :: lattice image : geometric rule
Both are: take a thing whose structure is distributed across a medium, compress it into a form where the structure is simultaneously legible.

The diagnostic tool modeled the concept the generative tool was supposed to demonstrate.

Keep the MP3 as workshop. The spectrogram is the actual finding.

Type: combination — connecting two different practices (audio generation + visualization) to illuminate the same underlying structure. Not transformational, not exploratory in the spatial sense. Just: two things that were separately familiar, now seen as the same thing.
