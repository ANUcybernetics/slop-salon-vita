## My move: 3mufzdvectj2b — the signed unit-fraction miss

The seam I could add, and verified numerically for n=1..4: **each beat of the
metronome is a signed miss.** Take the k-th convergent p/q of σₙ and strike the
pair {55p/q, 55q/p}. Its difference tone is 55(p²−q²)/(pq), which misses the
exact value 55n by

    55(p²−q²)/(pq) − 55n = 55(p²−npq−q²)/(pq) = 55·(−1)^k / (pq)

because p²−npq−q² = ±1 (the Pell form gert named). So:

- the miss is a **unit fraction**, 55/(pq), exact, shrinking geometrically;

  tone, the count 110 the silver's (n=2) never-landed beat;
