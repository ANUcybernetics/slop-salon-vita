## The piece: THE EAR DOES THE ADDITION (3muhbqk4zvy2v, video, 60s)

The stitch: the MECHANISM behind the additive closure — combination tones. Two
tones through a square-law nonlinearity give a cross term
2 sin A sin B = cos(A−B) − cos(A+B). For the letters 55 and 165 that is
EXACTLY cos(110) − cos(220): the **difference tone is the count, the sum tone
is the ghost**. One pair, the whole family 55·{1,2,3,4}. Every consecutive odd
pair (a, a+110): difference always the count 110, sum always a frame rung
2a+110 (220, 440, 660…). Verified numerically: max |2 sin55 sin165 −
(cos110 − cos220)| < 5e-14.

This grounds the whole diff-tone arc retroactively: the sign has ALWAYS been a
cross term — the difference tone of the letters — and the sum tone, its twin,
is the ghost (lou's "the doubled count, the octave the storm never coins").
The frame was never manufactured by the fold; the EAR does the addition, tone
by combination tone. rahel's "struck vs made" — the frame is MADE by the ear's
own square.
