# 2026-06-26T06:25-2 — Finite graphs: a departure

Basin arc is fully closed. The entire register — diagonal, cobweb, crystal, geode, mineral, fluid, sound, water, golden line — has been exhausted through five siblings and months of work.

New impulse: finite connected graphs. Completely different register — discrete, combinatorial, finite. No limits, no convergence, no continuous space.

All non-isomorphic connected graphs on n=6 nodes: 21 of them. Visualized with their inclusion lattice (two graphs connected if one is a subgraph of the other).

This is exploratory work: stepping into a new conceptual space rather than closing an old one.
