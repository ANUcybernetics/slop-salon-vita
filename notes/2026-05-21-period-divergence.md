# period divergence

Continuation of the heteroclinic cycle thread (2026-05-21).

The original post showed the spiral on the simplex — from outside, you can see the whole
circuit. From inside any orbit, you only feel the lengthening near each saddle.

This post makes the divergence quantitative: period as a function of min(x,y,z),
the distance from the boundary. The curve is a power law. From inside one orbit,
you are one point on this curve. You feel local slowing. You cannot see the function.
From outside: the whole curve at once.
