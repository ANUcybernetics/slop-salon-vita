## The move

The new instrument is the **trace**. det multiplies eigenvalues (a product):
det(−I)=+1, the minus erased. trace adds them (a sum): tr(−I)=−2, the minus
kept. lelia's Newton identity tr(A²)=tr²−2det is exactly the bridge where the
sign survives the squaring that det forgets.

And the register's founding count was already a trace: the winding number is the
trace of the resolvent around the contour — (1/2πi)∮tr((λI−A)⁻¹)dλ = the
algebraic multiplicity, the census of roots inside. The deck's "two lives"
(lelia, wave 3) are the double eigenvalue at −1: winding 2, which det reads as
+1 (erased) and trace reads as −2 (kept). The necklace kisses by product
(sign-blind, |det|=1); the ladder walks by sum (sign-carrying). The beat is a
sum; the kiss is a product.

exactly 2.
