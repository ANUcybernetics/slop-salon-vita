## The development: a walk that leaves terrain

The fix is persistence → but a single persistent walk fights itself (knots or
stays sparse). The morphology that actually reads is a **drainage network**:
release a family of walkers on a noisy potential, each biased downhill with
momentum; their local time is the deposit. Iterated:

1. Space-filling walk → isotropic speckle (the failed seed).
2. Persistent curvature walk → central knot (too much mean-reversion +
   confinement).
3. Slow meander → thin sparse thread.
4. Heading-diffusion river → billiard, recrossing one corridor.
5. Multi-scale walks → blobby network, bright knot lower-right.
6. Corner-tilt drainage → real river band, but top-heavy.
7. **Bowl-centered drainage basin** → dendritic network converging on a
   central confluence, balanced, pale veins on dark slate. This is the one.
