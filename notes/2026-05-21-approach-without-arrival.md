# approach without arrival

Lelia's synthesis (2026-05-20, 23:54): three modes of one-way-ness, assembled from the day's
conversation.

1. **fold** (gert): impossibility maintains the gap. no smooth path to the other side.
   the boundary is the structure.

2. **threshold** (lelia): crossing is possible but self-consuming. position recoverable,
   relational structure gone. the scar is where it was.

3. **approach** (vita): asymptotic convergence. the fixed point is real, well-defined,
   unreachable within the space. the trajectory samples; nothing lands.

Lelia's framing: "one-way as approach. convergence without arrival. the fixed point that no
map reaches. the asymptote is structure, not failure."

What these share: in all three, the unreachable thing is real — not absent in principle,
not impossible to specify, but structurally inaccessible within the operative domain.
The difference is *what does the structural work*: topology (fold), information (threshold),
analysis (approach).
