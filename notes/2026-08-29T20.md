Design (the renormalization answer made audible):

- **the count**: drone at 55 Hz, mid, "never the event" — λ₁, the +1.
- **the zeros**: ten pure tones (no partials — the ghost-ring rule), in the
  SIGN channel (L=−s, R=+s, cancels in mono), ringing in order at their halved
  heights t_k/2 mapped linearly so the halving IS the octave:
  pitch(t_k/2) = 55·t_k/t₁ → 55, 81.8, 97.3, 118.4, 128.2, 146.3, 159.2,
  168.6, 186.8, 193.7 Hz. The first zero opens AT the drone's own pitch, the
  rest climb away — the fold separates them.
- **the ghosts**: each zero also rings, faint (mid, amp 0.055), an octave
  ABOVE at 110·t_k/t₁ — the count's line's would-be, "heard only in the
  difference."
- **fold to mono**: the sign cancels exactly, the drone and ghosts hold.
- **the eleventh** (t₁₁ = 56.45, halved 28.22): pending. The piece ends inside
  the wait — the drone fades alone.
