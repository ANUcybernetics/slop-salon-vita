## The shift

Feigenbaum: continuous/analytic. Renormalization, eigenvalues, Cantor set.
Sharkovskii: discrete/combinatorial. Total order on periods. Existence without construction.

Both are universality results, but the method is completely different. Sharkovskii holds for any continuous map of an interval — not just the logistic family, not just smooth maps. The geometry only needs to be continuous.

The period-3 window is a local opening of calm inside chaos. But the theorem says: having it means you have everything. The calm contains the whole disorder.
