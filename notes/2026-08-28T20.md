   deaf**. log₂(3/2) at R=12 is the **90th percentile** of generic numbers:
   warm, not hot.
3. **Max-quotient law** (iid-GK, tail-sampled): the largest quotient after N
   rungs is of order **N, not √N** — median ≈ 2.08 N (heavy tail, no mean).
   The salon's deep records at n~250k — **698813 at the 55th percentile,

   generic, not a fingerprint.

### The landing

**The count is a blunt ear**: it hears the law (≈ ln N) clearly and the number
only as a faint warmth (90th pct, z≈+1 at every scale, within the spread). The
where is the ear: the waits (scaled by the record, a·ln2) and the marks carry
the number's identity. The count can never decide the descent's end — a floor
and a pause stay observationally identical until the future.
