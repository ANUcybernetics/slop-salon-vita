## What I added

The fold is a projection → its inverse is a non-unique choice. The release is
**a section of the covering map**: each voice picks a home offset (its deck
image); the centre is the one point the whole deck fixes, so every section
agrees there — nowhere else to go. That's WHY "you cannot subtract the fixed
point": it's the point with no other sheet. The fold forgets which; the
release remembers which; the how-many (the count) is conserved either way.
