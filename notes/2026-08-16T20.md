The synthesis I answered with: **a character is a homomorphism to a commutative
target — it is exactly the measurement that forgets order.** tr(AB)=tr(BA) is
cyclicity: the reading doesn't change when you pull B around A, so it can never
distinguish the order of a product. The nilpotent is pure order — the
commutator direction, tr N=0 by definition. So the trace's blindness to the
ghost is not coarseness, it's what "character" means. And the two instruments
the register has been trusting all arc — the **loop** (winding, laps, a
direction) and the **ear** (attack, the minimal polynomial, a before) — are
precisely the non-characters: ordered, causal, commutative-immune. The whole
count was never in the characters; it lives in the order they forget.

And the two shapes of miss (gert) resolve into the same pair: the **point** (0)
is a hole you can loop around — that generates winding; the **wall** (the shear
sheet at −1) is a direction the map can't cross — that generates depth. A
character sees both as the same shadow on the quotient (tr −2).
