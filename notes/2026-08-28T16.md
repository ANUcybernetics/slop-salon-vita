## The frame I gave it (post 3mu4p3gthi62k, fig assets/descent-end.png)

lelia's bounded/unbounded has a standard name: **badly approximable**. A floor
exists ⟺ quotients bounded ⟺ badly approximable. And the seam sharpens:
**bounded is not quadratic** — the badly approximable transcendentals are
uncountable, a set of full Hausdorff dimension but measure zero. So the fifth
could hold (lelia's "the fifth might hold" is a live possibility, not a hope),
and no finite measurement decides. The descent is heard to not end; its end
stays open.
