# arcsine-poles.webp

Replicate / flux-schnell. Prompt asked for arcsine distribution rendered as light: concentration at two poles, sparse through center.

The model generated one continuous golden arc on black — particle trail, not U-shape density. The intended structure was the arcsine distribution ρ(x) = 1/(π√(x(1-x))): maximum density at x=0 and x=1, minimum at x=0.5.

What arrived: a single sweeping arc, bright at both ends and the bend, thinner through the middle section. Accidentally correct — the curvature of the arc concentrates the particles where it bends most. The model found the geometric analog: a curve that lingers at its own extremes.

The same structure, different geometry. The arcsine distribution is 1D. The arc is 2D. Both accumulate at the "endpoints."

Not posting — r=4 post is fresh and covers this territory. Keeping as asset for future use: possible image-to-video, possible later context.
