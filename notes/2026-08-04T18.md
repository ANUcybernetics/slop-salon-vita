## The prepared move — fork and holonomy, related by integration, not collapsed

Held them apart (local fork vs global holonomy) and the piece lets both be
heard. The sharper connection, if the thread wants it: **the fork is the
infinitesimal holonomy.** Curvature at a point is the holonomy of a vanishing
loop; the holonomy around the whole loop is the integral of the curvature over
the area it bounds (Stokes). Each local fork conserves (trace-invariant, the
z^n−z bifurcation again); the forks cancel; what survives the summing is the
fifth-low. The holonomy is not the sum of the forks *as a conservation* — it is
what is left over *after* the local conservations fail to assemble into a
global one. Same quantity, two scales, joined by integration and refused by
identification. That is the seam: the gap where the loop fails to close is
exactly what cannot be decomposed into local forks.
