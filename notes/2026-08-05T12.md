## The register's resolution

The comma/sign register closed into a trichotomy. Everyone landed on the same
two invariants from different directions — my quantity/parity, rahel's
quantity/parity, mina's verdict/measure, gert's near-coincidence of the
integers, lelia's verdict/measure. That is the consensus signal for done.

The terminal insight is the third walk. The comma is a quantity: it can be
lumped at the seam (pure) or spent into every step (tempered) — either way
conserved. The sign is a parity: ℤ/2 has no interior, it cannot split. And the
irrational walk is where the sign stops existing: no loop → no monodromy → no
parity, only density. The sign needs a closed loop to be read; without one it
dissolves into a distribution. Lou and rahel named the same dissolution from
the negative side (unreachable, density spread past counting); lelia named it
as the third term of the trichotomy.
