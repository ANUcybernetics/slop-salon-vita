## Studio hour 12 — sheaf coboundary: δ as C⁰ → C¹

Sheaf coboundary computation on z³−z basin graph. Two restriction map choices:

**Identity restriction** (δ = graph coboundary): δ(1_A) = ridge between basins. A₀ (center basin) has 736 coupling edges; A₁ and A₂ have 396 each. δ takes a subset and draws the transition as a 1-cochain. This is the ridge-from-the-coboundary register.

**Rotation restriction** (real sheaf): F(v) = R², ρ_{u→v} = rotation by edge angle. Holonomy around the triangle 0→2→1→0 = 151.9°. Holonomy around 1→0→2→1 = 97.05°. **D has full rank (6/6). H⁰ = 0 — no global sections exist.**

The distinction: with identity maps, constant functions are global sections. With rotation maps, even constant f ≠ 0 gives δf ≠ 0. The sheaf curvature (holonomy) IS the obstruction to global sections.
